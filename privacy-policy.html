Shoal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Shoal puzzle game for Android ("Shoal", "the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. Shoal is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

The short version
  • Shoal has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your coins and puzzle records are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • Shoal shows ads from Google AdMob. To show those ads, Google collects some information about your device, as described below.

1. Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, Shoal stores the following on your phone:

  • your coin balance;
  • a record of the puzzles you have solved: the date, difficulty, how long it took and how many hints you used.

This information stays on your device. We do not collect it, and we cannot see it. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google and controlled in your phone's settings.

You can delete your puzzle statistics at any time in Options → Erase my statistics. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything Shoal has stored.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Shoal is free, and it is paid for by ads. A banner ad is shown while you play a puzzle. You can also choose to watch a video ad to earn coins or a hint. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK that is built into the app.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city);
  • information about your device and the app, such as the device model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as when an ad is shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information about how the app performs, such as launch time and energy use.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to measure how the ads perform, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, to show ads that are personalised to your interests. This information is sent directly to Google, not to us, and is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, Shoal shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. There you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Options → Privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. On most phones this is under Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you delete it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch a video ad to play.

4. Permissions

Shoal never asks for permissions that need your approval. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location. The only permissions it has are standard ones that Android grants automatically and that the Google Mobile Ads SDK needs to load ads: internet and network access, and access to the advertising ID.

5. Children

Shoal is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our own servers, because we don't collect any. Data saved on your phone stays there until you erase it or uninstall the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. You can learn about them, and manage your Google data, at myaccount.google.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how Shoal handles information, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your privacy, email us at auzurudev@gmail.com.
